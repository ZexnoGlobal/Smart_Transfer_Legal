Privacy Policy

Smart Transfer  •  Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Transfer ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use its file-sharing, phone-cloning, and device utility features.

Important: This policy should be reviewed against the actual release build and all enabled advertising or analytics SDKs before publication. Any practices described as optional apply only if the corresponding feature is enabled.

1. Information and permissions

Depending on which features you use and which permissions you grant, the App may access:

  • Files and media: photos, videos, audio, documents, and other files you choose to view, manage, send, or receive.
  • Installed application information: application names, package identifiers, icons, and APK files to let you select apps for transfer. Private app data is not included in APK transfers.
  • Contacts: contacts you choose to export for phone cloning, including creating a VCF file. Contacts access is requested only when required for that feature.
  • Network and device information: local-network connection details and device identifiers needed for discovery, pairing, and transfer; diagnostic information needed for network, battery, or storage utilities.
  • Camera: camera access when you choose to scan a QR code.

Android permissions can be managed or revoked in your device settings. Some features may not work without the relevant permission.

2. How information is used

The App uses information to display your selected content, connect devices, transfer files, export or import contacts, scan QR codes, show storage or network information, and maintain transfer history or preferences where those features are enabled.

3. File transfers and storage

Files you select for sharing may be transmitted to the receiving device through the transfer connection you establish. Recipients can access files you intentionally send. Received files and exported contact files may be stored on your device. Transfer records and preferences may be stored locally. Deleting the App or clearing its data may remove locally stored records, but files already exported, transferred, or saved elsewhere may remain.

Do not transfer sensitive files to devices you do not trust. The security of a transfer depends on the App's implemented connection and pairing methods; this policy does not claim end-to-end encryption unless it has been independently verified in the released build.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App may use Clever Ads Solutions (CAS) and its advertising partners to display advertisements. If advertising is enabled, these providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, device characteristics, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, subject to applicable law, consent choices, and their own privacy policies.

For more information, see the Clever Ads Solutions privacy policy. Other advertising network policies may also apply depending on which networks are enabled in the App.

We do not state that the App uses analytics, crash reporting, or cloud storage unless those services are actually included in the released version. This section should be updated if additional providers are added.

5. Sharing of information

Content is shared with another device when you initiate a transfer. Advertising providers may process data as described above if ads are enabled. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and services, to the extent applicable. We do not claim that user files are uploaded to our servers unless that behavior is implemented.

6. Data retention and deletion

App preferences and transfer history may remain on your device until you clear them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Files that you have saved, exported, or sent may need to be deleted separately from their storage locations or receiving devices. Advertising partners may have their own retention periods and privacy controls.

7. Your choices

  • Grant or deny optional permissions in Android settings.
  • Choose which files, apps, or contacts to transfer.
  • Clear local App data or uninstall the App.
  • Use device privacy and advertising settings to manage advertising identifiers and applicable consent choices.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has shared personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the concern. Advertising configurations should comply with the intended audience and applicable children's privacy requirements.

9. Security

We aim to handle data only as needed for the features you choose. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device updated and verify the recipient before sending files.

10. International processing

Third-party advertising services may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, or legal requirements. The latest version will display an updated date at the top of this page.

12. Contact
